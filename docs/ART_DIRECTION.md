# Art direction

**One idea:** you draw a little blob, it becomes a duckling, and it falls into line behind mother duck. Everything on the page serves that line.

## The page

A small park tile (20 × 15 m) floating on warm paper (`#f4efe4`), seen from MEADOW's elevated three-quarter camera, ending in the family's ragged dry-brush edge that breaks into specks. The wordmark sits top-left in Fraunces: **DUCKLINGS** / *draw one, it follows*.

## The park (scenery, not editable)

- **Pond** — about a third of the tile, two lobes joined by a narrow waist. A **gentle bank** on its near side (a soft sandy slope; the ducks walk in here) and a **steeper edge** elsewhere (a darker earthen lip, a few stones). Reeds in clumps, two lily pads, petals drifting on the surface, a dragonfly.
- **Bridge** — a little humped wooden footbridge across the waist, with a low rail. Walkable; you cannot enter the water from it.
- **Gravel path** — winds in from the near-left corner past the bench, crosses the bridge and wanders along the back.
- **Bench** — wooden slats on dark legs, beside the path.
- **Trees** — two round puff trees (one a spring blossom), with soft lavender shadow pools.
- **Flowerbed** — an oval of soil with butter-yellow and blush flowers, a pale edging.
- **Small life** — reeds swaying, a dragonfly, petals on the water, ripples from anything touching it, two clouds drifting along the edges. Nothing competes with the ducks.

## The characters

- **Mother duck** — an original picture-book hen: a round cream-and-buff body with a warm brown saddle and painted folded wings, a warm brown head, a soft orange bill, dot eyes with a catchlight, a perky tail that flicks. About twice a duckling's size, on short orange legs.
- **Ducklings** — round puffs of butter-yellow fluff (fine puffs plus two fur shells), a big round head, a tiny orange bill and feet, dot eyes with a catchlight, wing nubs. Per seed: a touch bigger or smaller, a darker cap of fluff, and now and then a cowlick tuft. They must all read as one family.

## Motion

- **Waddle** — the body rocks side to side, feet paddle quickly, a squash-and-stretch on each step; ducklings take about twice as many steps as mum.
- **Swim** — plop in (a tiny hop, a squash, a splash, a ring), bob, paddle with a V of rings off the tail. Climb out with a hop up the bank and a quick shake of the fluff.
- **The line** — snakes exactly through mum's curves; stretches a little when it speeds up, bunches when it stops, shuffles into a loose huddle around her when she rests.
- **Peep** — a tiny cream speech-tick by the head; neighbours answer in a ripple down the line.

## Reveal

MEADOW's cream pen line; on release the blob is painted in as fluff by brush strokes sweeping tail to nose (~1.1 s, half with reduced motion), flat colour first and then light; the shadow arrives at 60 %. The duckling blinks, peeps and hurries to the end of the line.
