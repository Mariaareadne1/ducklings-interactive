# DUCKLINGS

**Draw a duckling. It falls into line behind mother duck.**

A small painted park in the browser, floating as a tile on paper. Draw a little blob and it becomes a fuzzy yellow duckling that hurries to join the end of the line. Lead mother duck anywhere — around the bench, over the bridge, down the bank and *plop, plop, plop* into the pond — and every duckling toddles after her.

Sibling of [MEADOW](https://github.com/Mariaareadne1/meadow-interactive), [POND](https://github.com/Mariaareadne1/pond-interactive), HIVE, SKY and TIDE.
